# Squash Mode

Squash mode is the operating tempo: do the work in concentrated bursts, then rest, do not pretend you can sustain peak output for eight hours. WormHole's cron jobs are deliberately bursty. The dreaming pass at 03:00 is squash mode at the day scale: one big consolidation pass, then nothing until tomorrow. This page explains why bursty beats steady-state for an agent that runs unattended on a personal machine, and how to tune your own cron schedules to match the squash-mode pattern.
