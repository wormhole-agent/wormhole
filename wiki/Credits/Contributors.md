# Contributors

Tier 3: every human who has landed a patch. Generated from `git log` at release time by `tools/credits-tier3.js`. Each entry lists the contributor's name (or handle), their first-merged-PR date, and a count of merged commits. This page is the project's running record of who has shaped the code. New contributors are welcome; the entry point is `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
